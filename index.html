About Our Green Campus

Our college is dedicated to environmental stewardship and building a sustainable learning environment. Through extensive tree cover, energy-efficient architecture, and active student involvement, we maintain an eco-friendly campus that conserves natural resources and reduces our carbon footprint.

Green Campus Activities

  • Annual Tree Plantation and Biodiversity Drives
  • Zero-Plastic Campus and Reusable Bag Campaign
  • Eco-Club Cleanliness and Awareness Rallies
  • Cycle-to-Campus and Carpooling Initiatives

Solar Energy

Rooftop Solar Plant

Our campus is equipped with a 250 kW rooftop solar photovoltaic grid that powers academic blocks and administrative offices.

View Project

Clean Energy Impact

The solar grid generates clean electricity and prevents over 200 metric tons of carbon emissions every year.
