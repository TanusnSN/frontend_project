Project Overview

Commissioned across multiple academic blocks, this grid-tied solar photovoltaic installation supplies over 60% of our institution's peak daytime power demand. The setup runs silently, reduces dependence on conventional grid power, and exports surplus renewable electricity during semester breaks.

Technical Highlights

  • Capacity: 250 kW High-Efficiency Monocrystalline Array
  • Inverter: Dual String Smart Inverters with IoT Monitoring
  • Annual Yield: Over 300,000 Units (kWh) Generated
  • Emissions Cut: 240+ Metric Tons of Carbon Dioxide Offset Annually

System Architecture

Grid-Tied Photovoltaic Infrastructure

The system utilizes Tier-1 monocrystalline solar panels engineered with high low-light efficiency to optimize yield during monsoon and winter seasons across all buildings.

Real-Time IoT Monitoring

Smart energy meters track hourly power generation, cell temperature, and load distribution, feeding live performance analytics directly to the college environmental audit team.
